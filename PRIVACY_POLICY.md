# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

EduAssess / Adaptive Mastery is an educational assessment platform for students, teachers, and school administrators. This policy explains what data the product is designed to handle and the minimum privacy controls required before production launch.

## Data We Collect

- Account data: name, username, email, role, grade/class assignment, and school affiliation.
- Learning data: assessments, answers, scores, mastery progress, skill breakdowns, points, achievements, and micro-learning progress.
- Operational data: login/session metadata, audit logs for sensitive admin actions, notification status, and technical diagnostics.
- Optional media: profile image or question image attachments when enabled by the school.

## How We Use Data

- Provide adaptive assessments and personalized learning recommendations.
- Show progress reports to the student, teacher, and authorized school administrators.
- Secure accounts, detect abuse, and maintain audit trails for sensitive actions.
- Improve reliability, performance, and product quality.

## Data Sharing

Student data should only be visible to the student, assigned teachers, authorized administrators, and the school account owner. Data must not be sold. Any third-party service, such as push notifications, crash reporting, storage, analytics, or hosting, must be configured under a data-processing agreement before production use.

## Security

The platform uses authenticated API access, server-side role checks, secure local token storage on mobile, HTTPS-only production traffic, rate limiting, input sanitization, and audit logs for sensitive actions. Production deployments must not expose secrets in the app bundle, logs, repositories, screenshots, or support messages.

## Data Retention

Schools should define a retention period for student records, assessment results, audit logs, and uploaded files. Production deployments should support deletion or anonymization when a school contract ends or when deletion is legally required.

## Children and Education Data

Because EduAssess can process student information, schools must obtain the required permissions from guardians or authorized institutions according to the laws that apply in their country.

## Contact

For privacy requests, account deletion, or data access requests, contact:
