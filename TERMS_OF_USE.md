# Terms of Use

Last updated: May 2026

These terms define the baseline usage rules for EduAssess / Adaptive Mastery. They should be reviewed by legal counsel before public release.

## Purpose

EduAssess provides adaptive assessments, teacher tools, student progress tracking, micro-learning, notifications, and school reports. The platform is intended for educational use by authorized students, teachers, and administrators.

## Accounts and Access

- Users must sign in with an approved school account or an approved third-party login flow.
- Users must not share credentials or attempt to access another user's account.
- Administrators are responsible for assigning the correct teacher, student, classroom, and role permissions.

## Acceptable Use

Users must not:

- Upload illegal, harmful, or unrelated content.
- Attempt to bypass assessments, alter scores, or tamper with session data.
- Reverse engineer, abuse, overload, scrape, or attack the service.
- Use the product to expose student data outside authorized school workflows.

## Educational Content

Teachers and schools are responsible for reviewing questions, answers, media, lesson content, and reports before relying on them in formal education decisions.

## Availability

The platform may be unavailable during maintenance, network outages, hosting incidents, or third-party service failures. Schools should keep operational backup procedures for high-stakes exams.

## Privacy

Use of the product is also governed by the Privacy Policy in `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.

## Changes

The product owner may update these terms as the platform evolves. Production deployments should publish the active terms URL in the app and store listing.
